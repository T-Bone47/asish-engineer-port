# PRODUCT — Asish Oliver M Portfolio

The product constitution. Read this before touching layout, type or colour.
Visual decisions live in [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md).

## Identity

**ASISH OLIVER M**

- **Primary role:** MOTORSPORT SOFTWARE ENGINEER
- **Secondary role:** AI / ML ENGINEER
- **Supporting statement:** "Building software, simulation, telemetry and
  intelligent systems for engineering problems."

## What this product is

A personal engineering portfolio whose job is to make a technical visitor
believe three things quickly:

1. This person builds real systems, not demos.
2. The systems have depth — measurable, validated, honestly bounded.
3. The person who built them understands both motorsport and software.

It is **not** a design showcase, an AI-effects demo, or a marketing page.
The interface is evidence of the engineer's taste, not the subject of the
page.

## Audience (in priority order)

1. **Engineering recruiters** — need projects, evidence, contact, fast.
2. **Motorsport / race engineers** — need domain credibility and rigour.
3. **Software & AI/ML engineers** — need architecture and validation.
4. **Technical collaborators / hiring managers** — need scope and honesty.

A recruiter must reach projects, experience, skills and contact within two
clicks. Rich design must never become navigation friction.

## Voice

Technical · confident · curious · precise · engineering-first · understated ·
ambitious without exaggeration.

## Content truth policy (non-negotiable)

The portfolio's credibility depends on truth. Never invent:

- metrics, benchmark numbers, or test results
- experience, employers, or dates
- technologies not actually used
- users, clients, or social proof
- project status beyond what is genuinely known
- academic scores of any kind (no CGPA, grades, marks, semesters)

Where information is genuinely unknown, **omit it**. An empty list is
honest; a plausible placeholder is a lie that costs the whole portfolio if
found. Gaps are marked `TODO(owner)` in `data/projects.ts` so they are
visible in code review, never rendered.

Synthetic or illustrative visuals must be labelled as such. Only Live F1
Intelligence's measured figures (1,067,193 canonical events · 0 malformed ·
~2,650 events/s · ~0.10 ms p50/event · 99 MB peak) are verified evidence.

## Explicit anti-references

Things this site must never resemble:

- a generic AI-generated portfolio (centred hero, card grid, gradient glow)
- a SaaS dashboard or crypto landing page
- a cyberpunk / sci-fi HUD
- a template portfolio or component-library demo
- excessive glassmorphism, purple AI gradients, neon
- over-rounded card soup; card grids everywhere
- fake technical dashboards and fake terminal interfaces
- decorative statistics

## Content states

Every claim carries an explicit state: VERIFIED · ILLUSTRATIVE · ACTIVE ·
NEAR COMPLETION · IN DEVELOPMENT · PLANNED · PRIVATE. Unfinished work must
never be dressed as finished.

## Anti-references in layout terms

The single most reliable "this was generated" tell is repetition of the
*same* structure. Therefore: no section may repeat another's geometry, and
no element may appear in every section in the same role. See
[DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md) for the concrete rules.
